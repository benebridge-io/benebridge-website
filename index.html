Death claim verification creates regulatory and financial risk

Manual verification processes expose institutions to fraud losses, regulatory violations, and operational inefficiencies. Traditional workflows cost $200-$800 per claim with 2-4 week processing times.

Regulatory Risk

Manual verification creates audit trail gaps and regulatory exposure under GLBA §501(b). Inadequate fraud prevention can trigger examiner scrutiny and compliance violations.

Operational Liability

Staff verification errors cost institutions $15K-$500K per fraudulent claim payout. Manual processes don't scale and create workflow bottlenecks.

Integration Burden

Legacy verification workflows don't integrate with core banking systems, requiring manual data entry and creating operational inefficiencies.
